SIH Problem Statement SIH26044

Bridging Skill Gaps & Fostering Collaboration

A significant gap exists between academic learning and industry expectations. Students struggle to identify exact career pathways, while industries struggle to find job-ready talent.

Our platform creates a complete digital ecosystem. Through Industry Questionnaires & Skill Mapping, students identify gaps and complete verified courses. Simultaneously, Academicians get access to industrial training, FDPs, and consultancy projects to keep curricula aligned with market trends.

Core Objectives
  • Skill Questionnaire & Profiles: Evaluate technical and soft skills through industry-designed tests.
  • Internships & Job Opportunities: Match students to live projects based on verified skill scores.
  • Academician Growth: Dedicated portal for Faculty Development Programs (FDPs) and research projects.
